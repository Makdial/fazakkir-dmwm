{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The one player for the whole site. It sits outside the page content so it can keep playing while nav.js moves between pages: on a watch page player.js docks it into that page's [data-player-slot]; anywhere else it floats as a mini player. Nothing here is specific to a video. player.js fills in the source, the subtitle tracks and the title for whichever video is current. {% endcomment %}
{% comment %} No poster and no native controls. The custom control bar below takes their place, and the Media Session wiring in player.js is independent of the `controls` attribute, so background playback still works. {% endcomment %} {% comment %} Captions are drawn here rather than by the browser, so they can look the same on every platform and stay put in fullscreen. {% endcomment %}
